### Content Gaps

_These are things a cautious first-time buyer would want to know that are missing from the current deal page._

2. No service duration specified for any package — auto detailing can range from 2–8+ hours; customers need to know whether to plan to wait, drop off their vehicle, or schedule around a full-day service
3. No vehicle size or type pricing clarification — detailing pricing typically varies by vehicle class (sedan vs SUV vs truck), yet all three options show single prices with no mention of whether larger vehicles incur additional charges
4. No information about service format (mobile, drop-off, or wait on-site) — critical for customer planning and a standard question in auto detailing that determines whether they need alternate transportation
5. No business hours or appointment availability windows provided — customers cannot assess whether scheduling will fit their work/life schedule before purchasing the voucher
6. No cancellation or rescheduling policy stated — beyond the 180-day promotional expiry, there's no clarity on what happens if a customer needs to cancel or move their appointment
7. No vehicle condition requirements or service exclusions mentioned — some detailers refuse excessively soiled vehicles or charge supplements for pet hair, heavy staining, or oversized vehicles; customers need to know if their car qualifies
