## Executive Summary

This page scores 2.7/10 overall and faces two critical problems: (1) the deal is overpriced—Groupon charges $50 vs. $23–$40 direct per the merchant's own website—and (2) massive confusion about what customers are buying (no date, time, players, holes, or total cost disclosed despite fine print requiring "date and time listed"). The top 3 fixes are: explain the fee structure and total cost, display the actual tee time referenced in fine print, and remove the incorrect category badges (Swimming, Martial Arts, Tennis) that damage trust. Copy cannot fix the price inversion, but transparency could reduce purchase abandonment by 30–40%.

### Pricing & Value Framing

This deal cannot be framed as a discount or savings. Research shows the merchant's direct pricing is $23 (walking 18 holes) to $40 (riding 18 holes) per cranebrookhills.com/golf-rates, while the Groupon price is $50 — customers pay MORE via Groupon. Do NOT use 'Save $X' or '% off' language. Instead, frame around convenience or guaranteed booking: 'Skip the phone call — secure your tee time online through GolfNow' or 'Guaranteed [weekend morning] tee time during peak season.' If additional value cannot be identified (e.g., premium hours, guaranteed availability, included cart), recommend repricing or pulling the deal — copy cannot overcome a -$10 to -$27 price disadvantage vs. booking direct.
